# Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location (Unity Re-creation)

A self-contained, fully playable replica of *Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location* built in **Unity 6 (6000.6.8f1) with Universal Render Pipeline (URP)** using 100% procedural graphics rendering and procedural Web Audio / AudioContext synthesis.

## Features

- **Story Mode Nights 1–5**:
  - **Night 1**: Elevator, Primary Control Module (Ballora Gallery & Funtime Auditorium light/shocks), Circus Control Module.
  - **Night 2**: Angsty Teen voice glitches, Circus Control power outage, Under the Desk Bidybab tug-of-war, Ballora Gallery crawl with 4-stage proximity audio, and Breaker Room restart vs. Funtime Freddy & Bon-Bon.
  - **Night 3**: Funtime Auditorium dash vs. Funtime Foxy with flash beacon, Parts & Service maintenance on Funtime Freddy & Bon-Bon.
  - **Night 4**: Springlock suit survival with wiggling, faceplate opening, and climbing Minireenas.
  - **Night 5**: Circus Baby maintenance & keypad code input, Scooping Room Real Ending, and Private Room Fake Ending.
- **Both Endings**:
  - **Real Ending**: Scooping Room confrontation, S.C.U.P. execution, and bathroom mirror silhouette finale (Star 1).
  - **Fake Ending**: Accessing the Private Room with Ennard boss fight and *The Immortal and the Restless* Episode 4 (Star 3).
- **Circus Baby 8-Bit Minigame**:
  - Complete with Pink, Blue, and Green cupcake delivery, ice cream dispenser retrieval, and Elizabeth Afton capture (Star 2).
- **Custom Night Mode**:
  - All 10 Animatronics: Ballora, Funtime Freddy, Yenndo, Funtime Foxy, Bonnet, Bidybab, Electrobab, Lolbit, Minireena, and Minireena 2.
  - All 10 Challenge Presets (Angry Ballet, Freddy & Co., Funtime Frenzy, Dolls Attack, Girls' Night, Weirdos, Top Shelf, Bottom Shelf, Cupcake Challenge, Golden Freddy).
  - 7 Progressive 8-Bit Michael Afton decay cutscenes and Golden Freddy 10/20 V. Hard Fazbear's Fright Springtrap finale (Star 4).
- **Canonical Easter Eggs**:
  - 1/10,000 Technical Blueprints (Baby, Ballora, Freddy, Foxy).
  - 1/5,000 Lolbit mask in Primary Control.
  - 1/1,000 Rare spawns (Yenndo, Bidybab, Minireena).
  - Ballora Gallery 0+1 Blue Screen.
  - Slowed 1-5-7-8 whispers.
  - Popcorn bowl Minireena in TV Living Room.
- **Developer Debug Toolbar (`DEV DEBUG / RNG [▼]`)**:
  - Toggle 100% Easter egg spawn rate for instant testing.
  - Toggle Fast Timer vs. Canonical Timer.
  - Instant unlock all 4 stars and presets.
  - Quick-jump to any Night (N1–N5), 8-Bit Minigame, Private Room, Custom Night, or Finale.

## How to Run

1. Open project folder in **Unity 6 (6000.6.8f1)** or higher.
2. Open `Assets/Scenes/Main.unity` (or click `FNaF Sister Location -> Generate & Open Main Scene`).
3. Press **Play** ▶ in the Unity Editor.
